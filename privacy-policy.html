Privacy Policy

Victory Circle for iPhone · Last updated 27 September 2026

The short version: Victory Circle has no account, ads, analytics or tracking. Game data is stored on your iPhone. You choose whether to export a backup or share a result.

What the app collects

Victory Circle does not collect data from the app or send your games to us. It does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

What stays on your iPhone

Your profile, player names, game titles, rounds and scores are saved locally on your iPhone. They may also be included in a device backup you enable through Apple.

Backups you export

When you choose Export Backup, the app creates a JSON file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You decide whether to save it to Files, iCloud Drive or another destination. A copy outside the app remains in that destination until you remove it there.

Live Activities and sharing

Live scores on the Lock Screen are generated on your device. Share cards are images generated on your device and are sent only when you choose a destination from the share sheet.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from children or anyone else through the app.

Deleting data

Choose Settings → Erase All Data in the app to remove its local games and profile. Deleting the app removes its local data. Backups you exported or made through Apple must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you send so we can reply. You can ask us to delete that correspondence by emailing vsskkv@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, this page will show the new text and an updated date.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to vsskkv@gmail.com.
