Common questions

Quick answers

How do I add up points?

In a Points game, tap a player and type a number or a sum such as 10+5+3. The +5 and +10 buttons above the keyboard can help with common amounts. Save the round when you're done.

Why isn't my rivalry showing?

Rivalries count finished games. In the game, use ••• → Finish Game. Players are matched by name, so use the same spelling each time.

How do I see the score on my Lock Screen?

Start or open a game, then lock your iPhone. Check that Live Score on Lock Screen is on in Victory Circle's Settings and that Live Activities are allowed for the app in iPhone Settings. Dynamic Island display needs a compatible iPhone.

How do I move games to a new iPhone?

On your old iPhone, open Victory Circle's Settings → Export Backup and save the JSON file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, choose Settings → Restore from Backup and select that file. Restore replaces the games on the new iPhone, so export those first if you need them.

How do I fix a scoring mistake?

Tap Undo to remove the last round, or open Round History and swipe a round to delete it.

How do I delete my data?

Open Victory Circle's Settings → Erase All Data. This removes local games and your profile. Backups you exported to other locations must be deleted separately.
